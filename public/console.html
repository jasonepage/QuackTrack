QuackTrack Console

Organizer console

This is where the organizer runs the event: teams, keys, prompts, and the live log. Only the organizer can open it. Click the button, then use the same thing you set up with: Touch ID, your laptop password, or your organizer key.

Next step

First time with security keys? Read this.
  1. A security key is a small USB stick. Each team gets one. The key is the team: whoever holds it can see that team's prompt and sign for that team.
  2. To enroll a team, plug the key into this laptop, click Enroll with key on the team's card, and have the captain touch the gold circle on the key when it blinks.
  3. The browser may ask for a PIN. The captain picks one (at least 4 characters) and keeps it. A key that already has a PIN asks for that PIN.
  4. Wrong key? A red bar says so and nothing happens. Have them try again with their own key.
  5. Lost or broken key? Use Replace key on the team's card. You tap your own organizer key first, then the captain taps the new key. The word OVERRIDE shows everywhere so nobody can do it quietly.
  6. When a team is done, they tap in at the team page, tap once to see their prompt, and tap again to sign. Their card turns green.
