No account, no password, your key never leaves your hand.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Check out a key

    The organizer hands your team a key, or you bring your own with approval.

    Key A: yellow tag Key B: blue tag

  2. Step 2

    Tap to reveal

    Only your team's prompt appears, and the time is logged.

  3. Step 3

    Tap to sign

    Your submission is signed to its exact content. You get a receipt, and the console flips green.

    Not submitted → Submitted

What we store

Your team name, roster handles and roles, your prompt assignment, your submission name, repo, and hash, and log entries.

No prompt content goes in the log. No biometric data is stored. Your key signs on the device, and the private key never leaves it.
