QuackTrack

Support

Get help at the event

Go to the organizer desk, and bring your key with you.

Lost or broken key

Tell the organizer right away. Do not try to sign with another key. Another team's key or a spare key is refused, and nothing is submitted.

Common problems

  • The page says to open a different address. Open exactly the address it shows. Security keys only work on the address QuackTrack was set up for.
  • A red bar appears at the top. It says what happened and what to do next. Read it, then try again.
  • The page says your session ended. Tap your key to come back in.
  • You are on the shared laptop. Click Leave room when you are done, so the next team starts fresh.

Problems with the software

Open an issue on GitHub Issues.
